# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

- Leaders of SMBs and startups (CEOs, partners) whose teams already work with Claude Code, Codex or ChatGPT, and who want that context to serve the whole company.
- The jury of the Screenpipe hackathon "Build Agents That Remember" (42 Paris, October 2026), who evaluate the project in a few minutes.
- Then every employee: they install the client on their computer with a prompt pasted into Claude Code, then have nothing left to do.

## Product Purpose

The company brain gathers what each employee learns while working with their AI agents. Screenpipe captures the sessions on the computer, brain-sync distills them into knowledge (decisions, procedures, facts, tasks) and sends it to a single brain, split into zones by function. Everyone's agents query it through an MCP, with per-zone access. The dashboard shows the brain lighting up live when an agent fetches context from it.

Success: a leader understands in one minute what the product does and opens the demo; an employee installs it without help.

## Positioning

The context comes from real work with AI, captured on each computer by Screenpipe, not from a knowledge base to fill in by hand. The raw data stays on the computer, only the distilled knowledge goes up to the brain.

## Operating Context

- Demo: fictional company "Butine", connected scales for beehives (6 accounts, 34 knowledge items, 10 days of activity), one-click sign-in in demo mode (`/login`).
- Installing a computer: prompt generated by `/admin`, pasted into Claude Code, which runs a command (macOS, Linux or Windows): it installs `brain.mjs`, Screenpipe, the MCP and the hooks; `brain doctor` diagnoses a computer that is stuck.
- Company onboarding: guided 7-question interview and reading of the links provided (`/onboarding`).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Default zones: Dev, Product, Sales, Finance, Support, Leadership.
- Per-zone access: a member only sees their zones; a search on a closed zone returns nothing.
- Automation suggestions from repetitive prompts (fingerprint, without storing the raw prompt).
- Handover: a role's context taken over by the replacement, the employee can delete their items.
- Without a Claude key, everything works with local heuristics.
- Company skills: kept in the brain with their scripts, publishing reserved to authorized people, Skills tab, assignment of a skill to a cortex (zone) to give it only to those with access to that context; each computer installs its own in ~/.claude/skills.
- Pricing shown on the home page is indicative only; no payment is wired, managed hosting is not open yet.

## Brand Commitments

- Art direction set in `DESIGN.md`: monochrome "bone", phosphor reserved to what happens live, beads and lines, Space Grotesk.
- The 3D brain is the product's image.
- Tone: English, simple sentences, no emoji, no exclamation mark.
